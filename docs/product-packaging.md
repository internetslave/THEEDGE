# Product Packaging

## Positioning

EdgeIQ should be sold as a sports intelligence workspace for serious bettors and competitive groups, not as an "AI picks" toy.

The strongest message in this codebase is:

- trusted market context
- model-assisted interpretation
- accountable bet tracking
- community proof through leaderboards, comps, and tipping

## Best free vs paid structure

### Starter / Free

Use the free tier to prove trust and habit value quickly:

- live market board with trust labels
- AI match analysis access
- bet tracking and basic ROI history
- public leaderboard
- access to tipping and fantasy surfaces

Goal:

- let users understand the product
- let them log real outcomes
- create enough habit to want saved workflows and deeper analytics

### Paid v1: EdgeIQ Pro

The first paid tier should focus on workflow and sharper decision support, not billing theatrics:

- saved insight stack / watchlist
- advanced filters and faster slate triage
- richer analytics by confidence, value, AI-assisted vs manual, and sport
- stronger profile identity and premium leaderboard/community polish

Why this is the right paid v1:

- these features already exist in latent form in the frontend and data model
- they improve daily utility rather than just adding cosmetic access
- they increase retention because they make the product easier to come back to

## Premium features already latent in the codebase

- tracked bet history
- confidence metadata
- AI-assisted vs manual bet distinction
- value bet tagging
- leaderboard identities and streaks
- tipping rounds and comps
- fantasy assistant
- saved state in local storage and server-backed accounts

These are more monetizable than generic “more tips” promises because they create workflow, identity, and accountability.

## Later tier / expansion

After Pro is working, the next layer should include:

- private team rooms and invite-only comps
- saved views and scheduled digests
- alerts for price movement or match criteria
- export/reporting surfaces
- more advanced social/profile status systems

## Conversion notes

- keep the app careful and premium, not flashy
- sell clarity, trust, workflow, and proof
- avoid “guaranteed wins” framing
- show that EdgeIQ helps users make better decisions and measure outcomes honestly
